# Bitify 🎵

> A modern, fresh, high-fidelity music streaming application replicating and re-imagining the core capabilities of **BitChord**.

---

## ✨ What Bitify Does (Replication & New Features)

Bitify brings together music streams from **YouTube Music**, **Spotify**, and **JioSaavn** into a single, cohesive, high-performance experience with audiophile-grade DSP controls:

1. **Multi-Source Music Aggregator**:
   - Seamless streaming & catalog integration across YouTube Music, Spotify, and JioSaavn.
   - Per-song source badges and provider switcher filters.
   - Last.fm scrobbler integration.

2. **Fresh Neon Glassmorphic UI**:
   - Modern dark theme built on obsidian `#090A11` with electric violet (`#8B5CF6`) & cyber cyan (`#06B6D4`) accents.
   - Glassmorphism containers with frosted backdrop blur and glow effects.
   - Fluid typography and responsive layout.

3. **Audiophile DSP & 10-Band Equalizer**:
   - 10-band interactive frequency equalizer (60Hz – 16kHz).
   - Presets: *Bitify Dynamic*, *Bass Boost*, *EDM / Dance*, *Acoustic / Vocal*, *Rock*, *Flat*.
   - Sub-Bass Boost & 3D Spatial Virtualizer controls.
   - 24-bit/96kHz Lossless FLAC & Dolby Atmos indicators.

4. **Crown Jewel "Now Playing" Screen**:
   - Dynamic real-time blurred album art background.
   - Interactive seekbar with elapsed and total time.
   - Full playback controls: Shuffle, Repeat (Off/All/One), Skip, Previous.
   - **Live time-synced lyrics viewer**.
   - Up Next Queue bottom sheet with drag-and-drop reordering.
   - Sleep timer dialog (15m, 30m, 45m, 60m).

5. **Universal Search & Explorer**:
   - Real-time search across songs, artists, albums, and lyrics.
   - Source filtering pills (All Sources, YouTube Music, Spotify, JioSaavn).
   - Global charts (Global Top 50, Viral Hits, JioSaavn Weekly, Spotify Top 100).
   - Spotlight artists and curated genre tiles.

6. **Music Vault (Library)**:
   - Custom Playlist creator and management.
   - Liked songs vault.
   - Offline Downloads storage manager.
   - Chronological listening history.

7. **Floating Mini-Player**:
   - Persistent frosted-glass bottom player bar.
   - Top-edge real-time progress indicator.
   - Quick Play/Pause, Next, and Like controls.
   - Tap-to-expand transition into full Now Playing view.
